PLAYY.Music

PLAYY.Music is an all-in-one social music platform and ecosystem built for independent artists — by PLAYY. (formerly The Playground), a UK music company with a 15-year track record in PR, events, and artist development. The company has worked with artists including Ed Sheeran, Porter Robinson, FKJ, Pendulum, Röyksopp, Steve Aoki, Franz Ferdinand, and hundreds more.

The platform lets artists sell and stream music, license tracks for film and TV, sell merch and sample packs, run ticketed events and tours, crowdfund projects, access a music jobs marketplace, engage fans through groups, messaging, and video calls — all from one product, with real-time analytics and fair payouts.
